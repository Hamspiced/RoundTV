Before you press play.

  1. Open desktop Chrome or Edge.

    Web Serial needs HTTPS and a supported desktop browser. Phones, tablets, and Safari are not supported here.

  2. Plug in with a USB data cable.

    Connect directly to the computer. Close serial monitors and any other flasher tabs first.

  3. Confirm, connect, install.

    Select the Box’s serial port when asked. Keep it connected until installation completes, then reset or power-cycle if needed.

No port, connection failed, or stuck in download mode?

Try a known-good data cable and another USB port. If the port is busy, close serial apps and other browser tabs, then unplug and reconnect.

To enter download mode: hold BOOT, press and release RESET, then release BOOT and retry. The port name may change. Press RESET with BOOT released after installation if necessary.

Canceling the port chooser is safe; retry when ready. Do not disconnect during writing.

ABOUT THIS RELEASE

A stronger playback path. A clearer diagnosis.

R7 bounds late-frame dropping and enables faster FAT seeking, while keeping the ten-second audio buffer and 50% startup volume. Its controls show audio buffering, published-picture count, and video stage.

The reported freeze is not yet confirmed fixed on physical hardware. After updating, play a full episode. If it freezes, send two photos of the controls several seconds apart and note whether the V counter advances.
